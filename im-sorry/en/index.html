SINGLE INTRODUCTION

This song comes from a very real place. Life can feel crowded with too many voices—other people’s expectations, endless images, and thoughts that never stop moving. I used to think I had to make sense of everything, but I’m learning that not everything needs an answer right away.

That’s where “I’m sorry but I’m lazy” came from. It’s not really about being lazy—it’s about being tired of fighting every sound and allowing myself to drift. The song begins with “I stand in the center” and ends with “I leave the center.” I’m not running away; I’m learning what truly deserves my attention.

If the world feels a little too loud, take a few minutes to go nowhere. Just drift with me.
